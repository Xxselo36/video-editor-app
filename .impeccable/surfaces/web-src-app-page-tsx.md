# Landing (/) surface brief

Scope: the public landing page `web/src/app/page.tsx` (header, hero, sections, footer). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: short-video creators who film themselves on the phone (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), mostly visiting on a phone.
Action: create an account (label "Create free account" while billing is off, "Create account" once plans are live, "Start free" into the editor while accounts are off).
Proof: demonstration only (stock footage of one creator, shown as an example and never as a customer; no "Example" tag since Oct 2026); real caption styles drawn with the real caption fonts and preset values; no testimonials, numbers or claims the product cannot back.
Constraints: cleocuts-brand is binding (tokens, system font, command colours, LogoWord, i18n in 14 languages). Owner asked for "beautiful animations / 3D effects, no videos". Owner wants silence removal, filler-word removal and the stop/go commands visible, not only “Cleo cut”. Headline must be chosen per language (idiomatic), not translated literally.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one recording, laid out as a take strip in perspective; the visitor watches spoken commands and automatic cleanup remove pieces of it until a captioned 9:16 clip stands up out of the strip. It refuses the split hero with a rotating caption carousel, the icon-card bento and the numbered three-step timeline.

OWN-WORLD: CleoCuts dark violet surfaces (surface-0/1/2, ambient violet glow), system sans set bold and tight, violet only for the action and focus, and the fixed command colours as the only saturated hues: start blue, cut red, keep green, finish purple, stop/go amber. Strip segments are tinted surface blocks with waveform texture; command markers are thin coloured posts; flat chips narrate. Caption frames use the real caption fonts and preset colours.

STORY: Within one viewport the visitor understands that they record normally, talk to Cleo, and get a cut, captioned video back; then learns the six command words, sees what is removed automatically (bad takes, pauses, filler words, skipped sentences), sees real caption styles and the 9:16 reframe, learns they can restore anything in the editor, gets honest answers, and creates an account.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo left; pricing, language, sign-in right). Headline at display size, at most two lines on desktop, sub of at most 20 words, one primary button (brand-solid, rounded-xl, glow) visible without scrolling. Below, full container width, the stage: a horizontal take strip tilted back in 3D, a flat narration rail above it, a legend of what gets removed under it, and the finished 9:16 clip rising at the strip's right end; on phones the same stage at full gutter width directly under the button.

FORM: Take strip (concept A, rank 5 of 7 on the grounded list, owner-locked with additions); seed key 91c59b5e (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: the strip's cut-and-collapse loop with no pause control (owner, Oct 2026), stopped off screen and in hidden tabs and static under reduced motion; motion grammar: brand phase curve cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1), 240 to 600 ms steps, no bounce.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
